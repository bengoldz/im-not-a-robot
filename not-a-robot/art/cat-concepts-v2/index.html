Pick our cat, round 2

Round 1 was one cat in three outfits. These three are built differently from the ground up: different body structure, proportions and drawing style. Vote by number: 1, 2 or 3. The current cat is at the bottom for comparison.

1

Mochi

A squishy rice-cake of a cat: head and body are one soft bean, no neck, giant eyes and four tiny nubs. Cream coat with one big chocolate saddle.

  • One bean shape, no neck
  • Hand-inked, thick-to-thin outline
  • Cream + chocolate saddle

How it would animate

Squash and stretch. Each step is a little bounce: the bean flattens on landing and stretches long on the push-off, the nubs shuffle underneath, and the ears jiggle one frame late.

In-game size, next to the robot on the indoor tile

2

Noodle

A long, lanky slate cat: a body 2.5 times longer than tall on stilt legs, a sharp triangle head with tall ears, yellow almond eyes and a curling whip tail.

  • Long low torso, stilt legs
  • Crisp angles, tapered brush lines
  • Slate + white diamond + yellow eyes

How it would animate

Slinky stalk. The long legs swing from hip and shoulder pivots while the torso glides almost level; the whip tail curls and uncurls a beat behind; the head bobs low like a hunter.

In-game size, next to the robot on the indoor tile

3

Origami

A cat folded from teal paper: only flat triangles and quads, a light and a dark facet on every fold, coral ear flaps and solid diamond eyes.

  • Flat polygons only, zero curves
  • Facets instead of shading
  • Teal paper + coral ears

How it would animate

Stop-motion snap. Parts rotate on their fold lines in crisp held poses (no smooth tweening), like paper being re-folded; a jump is a quick fold flat, then a pop open.
